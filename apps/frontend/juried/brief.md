# Brief: CCD operator dashboard

**What it is.** The control center of a crowd density mapping and anomaly detection platform. CCTV
cameras feed a detector and tracker; the dashboard shows live cameras with heatmap overlays, a 3D and
plan map of the facility with a live density field, alerts with snapshot evidence, analytics,
notification history, system health and administration.

**Who uses it.** Venue security leads, transit control rooms and event safety officers, often on a
wall display for a whole shift, sometimes on a phone in the field. A read-only guest view exists for
partners.

**What they must feel in ten seconds.** Calm, legible instrument panel: where density is building,
which alerts need a decision, whether every camera and service is healthy.

**Art direction.** "Crowds are terrain": the isoline (contour of equal density) is the signature.
Dark graphite ink (#0c0e0d) panels with hairlines, warm paper text (#ece8de), one sodium-amber accent
(#f2a93b) for the active item, primary actions and key numbers. Density and severity colours are data,
never decoration. Archivo for interface and display, JetBrains Mono for every number, timestamp and
small label. No glass, no glow, no gradients. The facility map is the one place 3D is allowed.

**Content.** Real product data from the demo facility (a two-floor station simulated with moving
people); no placeholder text.

**Pages in the sheets.** Login, overview, live cameras, facility map, alerts, analytics, cameras admin,
notifications, system health, settings, users. Desktop at 1440×900 (first screen of each page) and
mobile at 390 wide (full page).

**Judge it as an operations tool, not a marketing site:** density of information is expected; the
question is whether it is ordered, legible and distinctive.
